Mumbaiमुंबई

Walk the streets of Colaba to Borivali, drive a car, a kaali-peeli taxi or an auto, and ride the Western and Central local trains station to station, fly a helicopter or take a speedboat. Distances are compressed to a fifth so a ride between stations takes seconds, not minutes. No missions.
